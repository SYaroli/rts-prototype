Command the defense

Select: left-click or drag a box; Shift adds units. A selects everyone.

Move: right-click terrain (or select units, then tap terrain on touchscreens). Units follow traversable ground; cliffs need a ramp.

Outpost sight: the standing outpost reveals enemies within 12 map units with a clear line of sight, even if nearby defenders have died. Terrain still blocks sight.

Targeting: tanks automatically prefer enemy armor they can hit. Right-click a visible enemy to override; a red ring marks the target. Mobile units approach it, while siege guns hold position. S or a ground move clears the order; a destroyed or unseen target restores automatic fire.

Return fire: idle infantry hit by an enemy may advance up to 2 map units from their response starting position to get a shot, then stop moving once they can fire. Repeated hits do not extend that limit. H toggles Hold Position; held squads still fire at enemies in range. Move and manual attack orders override the stance.

Collision: squads and vehicles occupy space, queue at bottlenecks, and steer around each other where room allows. Deployed tanks stay fixed.

Siege: select tanks and press E. Deployment takes 2 seconds. Siege guns cannot move, fire slowly, and cannot hit targets inside the inner ring.

Terrain: cyan rings show weapon reach, dashed rings show minimum range. High ground grants +2 sight and weapon range. Rock can block a shot even inside the circle. Infantry spot for siege guns.

Camera: wheel or +/− to zoom, arrow keys to pan (or W/D and Shift+A/S). A selects all and S stops units. Middle-drag pans. Space pauses. ⌖ recenters.

Compare: pick a preset before launch, or arrange your own force. Reset restores your launch position. Toggle the flank during deployment to change the route, keeping the enemy force unchanged.

This is a mechanics prototype: simplified models, deterministic attacks, no campaign or resource economy.
